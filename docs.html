02. The 6-Step Procurement Reconciliation Workflow

The core operational pipeline transforms messy, unstructured vendor quotes into cryptographically verified, ERP-ready purchase orders through six deterministic stages:

1 Create Sourcing Request

Define the Request Title, target delivery date, jobsite delivery location, and BOM line items with exact MPNs and ASTM metallurgical specifications (e.g. ASTM A516-70 Normalized Cryo).

2 Upload Supplier Quotations

Upload multiple vendor bids in PDF, XLSX, CSV, or DOCX. ForgeOps validates magic bytes, inspects file size, stamps SHA-256 digests, and initiates optical parsing.

3 Optical Evidence Inspection

Every extracted number is anchored to exact pixel coordinates on the source quotation with confidence scores. Non-destructive manual corrections preserve the original value in the audit trail.

4 ASTM Metallurgy Verification

The automated engineering radar checks quoted steel grades against operating temperature requirements. Quoting commercial carbon steel (ASTM A105) for cryogenic service triggers an immediate safety disqualification.

5 Deterministic Landed Cost Matrix

Incoterms normalization unifies EXW, FCA, and DDP quotes. Freight drayage, port handling, and customs tariffs are calculated deterministically to expose true landed cost and EBITDA savings.

6 Dual-Signoff & PO Dispatch

Separation of duties requires both Buyer commercial endorsement and Operations VP authorization before transmitting structured JSON/XML payloads to Oracle NetSuite, SAP S/4HANA, or Odoo 17.

03. ASTM Metallurgy & Cryogenic Safety Reference

🛡️ Why General AI Hallucinations Cause Catastrophic Failures

General LLMs treat ASTM A105 and ASTM A516-70 as generic "carbon steel". However, in subsea LNG and cryogenic service operating below -29°C (-20°F), standard A105 undergoes a ductile-to-cleavage transition. Under pressure, it shatters like glass.

ForgeOps AI enforces strict compliance with ASME B31.3 Section 323.2 and mandates Charpy V-Notch impact testing at -46°C (27 Joules minimum) for all cryogenic line items.
